Agave endures. An ember keeps the fire.

Agave
A plant that thrives where little else can. It gives us tequila, mezcal and nectar, and it stands for strength and resilience.
Ember
The glow left after the fire. Small, but it still holds heat and can light the next one. It stands for passion that lasts.

Together, that is the kitchen we set out to build. Sauces made by hand, recipes that hold on, and a dining room where the whole family can slow down.

“A higher scale Mexican restaurant, where you can come and feel like you’re home.”

Blanca Basinger, owner
